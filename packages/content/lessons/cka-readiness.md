CKA is hands-on and time-boxed. The largest domain is troubleshooting, followed by architecture, services and networking, workloads, and storage. Passing requires command fluency and a repeatable diagnostic sequence.

Prepare by doing, not reading. Practise creating, scaling, updating, exposing, and repairing workloads. Then practise reading events, logs, endpoints, claims, and node state. Know how to inspect control plane Pods, take an etcd backup in a real cluster, check certificate and kubeconfig identity, and repair a Pending Pod or broken Service.

Use the lab pool as a drill, not a memorised answer set. After each task, say the first diagnostic command and the evidence that would prove success. The readiness dashboard should show task evidence in every domain, especially troubleshooting.

Failure modes: jumping to a fix before scoping the failure, ignoring storage and installation domains, and relying on copied YAML. Verification: complete timed labs, explain each change, and confirm the final state with the required command.

# Proposal

## Why

Practice answers advanced the due date but not the repetition count, so correct
answers never progressed beyond the 3-day interval.

## What Changes

- Track successful repetitions on review cards.
- Compute intervals from repetition count.
- Keep lapses and failed answers resetting the sequence.
- Add deterministic schedule tests.

## Impact

- Review card model, exam review-card creation, practice answering, tests.

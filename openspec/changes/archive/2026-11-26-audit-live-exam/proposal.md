# Proposal

## Why

A live-exam audit found that lab timing could be extended by background tab
throttling, knowledge review links could show stale lesson titles, and running
exam states lacked accessibility and mobile coverage.

## What Changes

- Use an absolute deadline for the lab timer.
- Resolve question lesson titles from the current lesson metadata.
- Add axe and mobile overflow tests for running knowledge and lab exams.

## Impact

- Lab exam timer, question mapping, exam review links, E2E coverage.

# Proposal

## Why

Certification tasks still reused lesson scenarios, banks were modest, and content
integrity relied on manual review. This change makes exam scenarios independent,
expands the question bank, and adds automated curriculum checks.

## What Changes

- Replace lesson-sourced task scenarios with dedicated exam scenarios and checks.
- Expand the certified question pool with workload, governance, toolchain, and security questions.
- Add automated prerequisite, self-reference, task-pool, and scenario integrity tests.
- Re-run full validation and update docs.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `certification`: dedicated exam scenarios and larger banks.
- `content-authoring`: automated curriculum integrity checks.

## Impact

- `packages/content` task seeds, question bank, and tests.
- Docs and validation.

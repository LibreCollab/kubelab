# Proposal

## Why

The content audit found that 38 micro-lessons, 60 shared knowledge questions, and
16 tasks per hands-on track were not enough for five-certification preparation.

## What Changes

- Add 16 cert-focused lessons, bringing the teaching set to 54.
- Expand every teaching lesson to 150-250 words with failure modes and verification.
- Replace shared knowledge pools with 100-question KCNA and KCSA banks.
- Allocate exam questions by official domain weights and balance answer positions.
- Add nine tasks to each hands-on track, bringing every pool to 25 tasks.
- Map the new lessons and tasks into certification domains.

## Impact

- Lesson content, curriculum, knowledge banks, exam selection, task pools,
  certification roadmaps, tests, and documentation.

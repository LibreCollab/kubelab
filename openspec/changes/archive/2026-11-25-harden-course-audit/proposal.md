# Proposal

## Why

A full audit found lesson hints that could not satisfy their own checkpoints,
hands-on pools that mixed certification tracks, a Secret listing crash, an
unsupported CronJob describe, and accessibility and readiness presentation bugs.

## What Changes

- Make every lesson solvable from its documented commands.
- Restrict CKA, CKAD, and CKS pools to their own tasks.
- Fix typeless Secret listing and generic workload describe.
- Make all lesson pages pass serious axe checks.
- Replace the single-snapshot readiness bar with an explicit first-session state.
- Add regression gates for lessons, task solutions, route rendering, and a11y.

## Impact

- Lesson specs, task pools, roadmap mappings, simulator output, lesson theory CSS,
  cluster status accessible name, readiness trend UI, tests, and audit docs.
